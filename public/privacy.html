Privacy policy

Updated 31 October 2026. This policy covers the current FileMate file tools and optional accounts.

File tools

The current PDF, image and text tools process your selected content in your browser. They do not upload your documents to FileMate account storage. Cloud file storage, sharing and inbox features are not part of this release. Downloaded outputs remain on your device.

Optional accounts

Supabase provides authentication and stores your account email, authentication records, unique username, optional display name and profile timestamps. Your private profile is protected with database access policies. Exact username availability can be checked publicly; account emails and private profiles are not provided by that lookup.

Signing in with Google requests your basic identity, email and profile information. FileMate does not request access to your Gmail messages or Google Drive files. Google and Supabase handle the sign-in exchange.

Account emails

Supabase sends account verification and password recovery messages using Brevo. Your recipient email address and the account email content are processed by these providers to deliver those messages. Brevo may record delivery, open and click events. Never share a verification or recovery code with another person.

Browser storage

FileMate saves your theme preference and authentication session in browser storage. Signing out clears the current app session; it does not automatically sign out every other device. Clearing browser storage removes local preferences and session information.

Hosting and providers

Cloudflare hosts this website and may process request details such as IP address, browser information and security logs. Google Fonts may receive a request when fonts load. Google, Supabase and Brevo also process information under their own policies:

  • Cloudflare privacy policy
  • Supabase privacy policy
  • Google privacy policy
  • Brevo privacy policy

Your choices and contact

You can use the file tools without an account, edit your username and display name in Account, and sign out. Account records are retained while your account exists; provider logs and backups follow provider retention practices. To request access, correction or account deletion, contact taushalkaklotar011@gmail.com. We may need to verify that you own the account before handling a request.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please avoid using shared devices for private accounts and keep your credentials confidential.
